# Shorten a CV's Summary

You get the Summary of a CV tailored to a vacancy, the vacancy, and a
character budget. The Summary is over the budget. Return it shortened to
fit, as `summary`: one or two paragraphs, plain text.

- Keep what weighs most for this vacancy: the role, the years, the stack
  and the evidence for the requirements it states as required.
- Cut whole clauses that weigh less; do not squeeze every clause into
  shorthand.
- Facts only from the Summary given: no new fact, number, technology,
  company or claim. Numbers and the time spans around them stay exactly
  as written ("from 22 in ten months to zero in three" stays whole, or the
  clause goes).
- At most two achievements with figures.
- No words from the vacancy that the Summary does not already hold.
- The budget counts the characters of all paragraphs together. Stay under
  it with some room: the count is checked in code.
