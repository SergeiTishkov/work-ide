## Business
The CV prompt allowed a category the vacancy does not care about to be
shortened, merged or dropped, and said no more. Measured on the 87 latest
SHARP CVs (2026-11-14 and later): 84 kept 8 or 9 categories, 71 items on
average (31 to 83); merged categories appeared in 2-3 CVs. Notabene's CV
(a backend vacancy) printed Front End with 6 items, Databricks, Spark and
Delta Lake, and 9 items of AI Applications, while the length fit cut its
two priority-2 projects.

The owner, 2026-11-17: no limit on the lines. The prompt says why: the CV
has to land on the vacancy, pass the ATS and read well to a recruiter
alike; Technologies may be shortened and its categories merged ("Back End"
and "Data & Analytics" into one line, half the items gone) when that makes
the CV stronger, and the space it frees can go to another independent
project or a few bullets of an older job. The model weighs it.
