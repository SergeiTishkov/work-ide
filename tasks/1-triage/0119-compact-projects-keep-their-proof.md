# 0119 A compact project keeps its own result; a project that alone answers a must-have stays

Source: the agent, 2026-11-17 (Notabene's CV of task 0118, read with the owner)
Scope: config, tools

## Business
Notabene's CV (task 0118, V5, 2 pages) printed the independent projects
in the compact form, one line each. The model chose CHAD and the
Multi-Agent AI Engineering Framework at priority 1, the freight workspace
and the crypto chatbot at priority 2; the length fit cut the two at
priority 2. The owner agreed the choice fits the vacancy: it asks for
engineering with coding agents in a regulated product, not for building
LLM features.

What the compact lines lost:
- the framework's own measured result, the parent CV's "the panel caught
  two blockers that a 20-minute, four-agent build (183 passing tests) had
  missed": the strongest answer to the vacancy's "review their output as
  hard as a human pull request; you know where they fail";
- CHAD's audit logs and policy, which answer the must-have on
  least-privilege access;
- the AI-Powered News Aggregation Platform, the CV's one piece of
  evidence for its own AWS deployment (the must-have "end-to-end
  ownership in the cloud"); AWS stayed in Technologies only.

## Open question for the owner
1. A compact project line keeps the project's own measured result when
   the parent CV gives one (the framework's blockers, the freight
   workspace's 20 registrations a month).
2. A project that is the CV's only evidence for a must-have keeps at
   least one line: priority 1, or a single line instead of a cut.

Both apply to new versions; nothing is regenerated unless the owner asks.
