Source: the owner, 2026-10-28, closing task 0080

## Business

make an office job look remote. Checked on 2026-10-28: the wrong picks came
from a rebuild without the AI step, and task 0078 already keeps such
vacancies out of the list; the patterns were not needed. The owner did not
want it in cancelled: cancelled is for work the owner decided not to do,

The rule: a task whose problem is solved by other work, or whose purpose
is reached in another way than its items say, goes to done. Its text is
rewritten first, so a later agent does not take the proposed measures for
work still owed: the proposed measures are removed, the Business says why
the task needs no change of its own and which work solved it, and the items
are what was checked.
