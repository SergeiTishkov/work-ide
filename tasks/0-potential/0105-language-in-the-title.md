# 0105 Does a language in the title stress expertise?

Source: the owner, 2026-10-28, with the language rule of the sharp identity
Scope: identity:sharp

## Business
The sharp identity's rule since 2026-10-28: a remote development role in a
main language other than .NET/C# is a stretch unless the posting stresses
expertise in that language (years in it, expert, deep, strong); then it is
a "no". A language named in the title alone ("Python Engineer", "Senior
Java Developer") does not count as stressing it, for now. The owner was not
sure: such a title may well mean the expertise is wanted.

The check: the owner looks at real selections where a vacancy with a
programming language in its title was kept as a stretch, and decides
whether the title should count as stressed expertise (a "no") or stay
allowed.

## Implementation
If the title should count: a line in "Another main language" of
`sharp_identity.md` and the brief rewritten; the vacancies already read
stay as they are unless the owner asks for a re-read (task 0103).
